What’s behind the desktop your people sign in to?

Six things, working as one.

Most organizations buy these from six vendors and hold the pieces together themselves. Safe Workspace is all six in a single managed relationship.

  1. What’s protected

    Security

    Every access is verified. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. And the whole environment is held to a published security baseline that is applied and kept free of drift.

    Every plan comes with Microsoft Defender endpoint protection, through its Microsoft 365 license.

  2. Monitoring

    Automated platform monitoring, 24x7. Patching and scaling run on schedule.

    No one files a ticket for a patch.

  3. How the day runs

    The desktop

    A Windows desktop in the cloud, delivered on Azure Virtual Desktop. Every desktop is built to the same standard.

    Your files and settings follow you in a managed profile.

  4. Microsoft 365

    Mail, files and meetings, on the license that comes with your plan: Business Premium, E3 or E5. Passthrough, no markup.

    One less agreement to manage.

  5. Support

    One point of contact. One owner for every issue, from the first call to the fix.

    People, not a queue.

  6. What you can prove

    Compliance controls

    Technical controls that support the frameworks your plan covers, configured and kept in place.

    Six frameworks, by plan.
