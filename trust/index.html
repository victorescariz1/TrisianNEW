We run a workspace that is secure, reliable and provable, on terms you can read in advance. This page is the provable part.

Five questions a security team sends

  • What certification do you hold?

    ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

    Certified by an accredited certification body. The internal audit and the certification audit both closed with zero non-conformities. The scope names Safe Workspace.

  • What do you support, as opposed to hold?

    Six frameworks, by plan.

    Holding a certification and supporting yours are different statements. We hold one. Our controls support your obligations under six.

    What Trisian holds, and what its controls support
    We holdISO/IEC 27001:2022
    Our controls support your obligations underAdvancedCIS Controls v8.1 · NIST CSF 2.0 · HIPAA
    Enterprise addsSOC 2 · ISO/IEC 27001 · HITRUST

    Our controls support your obligations under these frameworks. Certifications and attestation reports are issued only by your independent certification body, CPA firm or assessor.

  • Who is responsible for what?

    The technical foundation is ours. The program is yours.

    We set up and configure the platform, remediate findings and keep the controls in place. Continuous evidence collection is available on every plan. Your policies, your training and your auditor stay with you.

  • Whose tenant is it, and who can reach it?

    Yours.

    Your tenant belongs to you and is separate from ours. Access is tiered, and no named Trisian user holds standing global administrator rights in it.

  • Do you work the way you ask us to?

    Yes.

    Trisian has no office and no datacenter of its own. The company runs on the same managed cloud model it sells.

Nothing is claimed before the document that proves it is issued.

The certificate details, our policies, our controls and our subprocessors are on the Trust Center. Some documents are public; others are shared on request, under a non-disclosure agreement.
