Plans

Three plans. One price per user.

Core, Advanced and Enterprise. Each builds on the one before, and each has one price per user, per month. Every plan starts at 50 users.

The three plans compared

  1. Core

    The secure, managed workspace every plan builds on.

    Microsoft 365 Business Premium · 50 to 300 users

    What you get

    • One standard image, centrally managed
    • A published security baseline, kept free of drift
    • Automated patching and scaling
    • 24x7 automated platform monitoring

  2. Advanced

    For organizations with a framework to answer to.

    Microsoft 365 E3 · 50 or more users

    Everything in Core, and

    • Advanced email security and email authentication
    • Infrastructure health monitoring

    Our controls support CIS Controls v8.1 · NIST CSF 2.0 · HIPAA

  3. Enterprise

    For organizations that answer to several.

    Microsoft 365 E5 · 50 or more users

    Everything in Advanced, and

    • A named Technical Account Manager from Trisian, assigned to your account
    • User experience monitoring
    • Centralized threat detection and response
    • Dedicated host pools and Zero Trust architecture

    Our controls also support SOC 2 · ISO/IEC 27001 · HITRUST

Questions

  • Where’s the price?

    Each plan has one price per user, per month. We quote it in writing with your assessment.

  • Which plan fits?

    The assessment recommends it.

    It looks at where you stand on security, performance and compliance, and at what you answer to.

  • What happens outside business hours?

    It depends on the plan.

    Standard support runs Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, your local time. On Advanced and Enterprise, critical and high-impact incidents are covered 24/7. Platform monitoring is automated and runs 24x7 on every plan. Coverage is confirmed in your proposal.

  • Why does Core stop at 300 users?

    Microsoft’s limit, not ours.

    Microsoft 365 Business Premium stops at 300 seats. Past that, you move to Advanced.
