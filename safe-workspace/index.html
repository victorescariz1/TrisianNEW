The six parts

  • The desktop

    A Windows desktop in the cloud, built from one standard image. Desktops are non-persistent: your files and settings follow you in a managed profile, and nothing else lives on the desktop.

  • Microsoft 365

    The license that comes with your plan. Passthrough, no markup.

    Business Premium on Core, E3 on Advanced, E5 on Enterprise.

  • Security

    Every access verified, data encrypted at rest and in transit, CIS-hardened images, and a published baseline kept free of drift.

    Dedicated host pools and Zero Trust architecture on Enterprise.

  • Monitoring

    Automated platform monitoring, 24x7. Patching and scaling run on schedule.

  • Compliance controls

    Controls that support the frameworks your plan covers. We own the technical foundation; your program and your auditor stay yours.

  • Support

    One point of contact, one owner for every issue, a service desk from Level 1 to Level 3.

    A named Technical Account Manager from Trisian on Enterprise.

We build it before we run it.

  1. Assess

    We look at where you stand on security, performance and compliance, and recommend the plan and the gaps to close.

  2. Build

    We build your workspace on the curated foundation and shape it to you: your applications, your desktops, our security recommendations, the rules you answer to. Building is a project: scoped, quoted in writing, and finished.

  3. Run

    From then on it is Safe Workspace: we support what we built, including what those projects put in place, inside a scope agreed in writing. When your environment needs to change, that is a project too, quoted before it begins.

Projects reach as far as the workspace does: moving you onto the platform, building it and its security, closing compliance gaps, and planning for the unexpected.

Where it lives

  • Where does it run?

    In your own cloud.

    We don’t host anything. Your workspace is built in your own Azure tenant, on your own subscription, and delivered on Azure Virtual Desktop.

  • What if there’s already a datacenter?

    Then we connect it.

    Your workspace runs on Azure Virtual Desktop, joined to your datacenter by a private connection: one you already have, or one we help you put in place.

We support what we build.

Managed Scope is everything we take operational responsibility for, across infrastructure, security, compliance and support. Inside it, every issue has an owner and a path to resolution. Outside it, the line is drawn in writing. You know in advance, not during an incident.

  • We own the escalation. If it starts inside the scope and we can’t resolve it alone, we carry the escalation and the communication.
  • The boundary is set in writing. If it starts outside (a third-party application, hardware, an internet provider), you knew that at onboarding.
  • Scope doesn’t move mid-contract. Additions go through a formal change order, documented and priced before work begins.

Outside every plan: application-level support for business software, hardware, software development and telecommunications. We run the infrastructure and operating system your applications need.
