Projects

Every project, in writing first.

Projects are how your workspace gets built, and how it changes afterwards. Each one starts from what you need, and is scoped and quoted before any work begins.

Six kinds of work.

This is where the work is, not a fixed list. If yours is not here, tell us what you need and we will scope it.

  • Migrations

    Getting onto the platform, from wherever you are today.

    • Safe Workspace migration The full move onto the platform, with your Microsoft 365 tenant where it needs to move too.
    • On-premises desktops to the cloud From the servers in your building to cloud desktops.
    • Citrix to Azure Virtual Desktop Off Citrix and onto Azure Virtual Desktop.
    • Multi-site rollout More sites and acquired companies, brought onto the same workspace.

  • Platform

    Designing and building what the workspace runs on.

    For Enterprise clients: multi-region infrastructure, designed and quoted for each client.

    • Safe Workspace deployment The workspace itself, designed and built around your applications and your people.
    • Azure infrastructure The Azure environment in your own tenant that the workspace runs in.
    • Microsoft 365 tenant Your Microsoft 365 tenant, designed and built with the workspace.

  • Security and identity

    Building the defenses around the workspace.

    • Privileged access How administrator access is granted, and to whom.
    • Endpoint detection policies The endpoint detection policies in your Microsoft 365 license, designed and configured.
    • Data loss prevention Data loss prevention policies, designed and configured.
    • Device management and web filtering Management of the devices that reach the workspace, with web filtering.

  • Compliance

    Closing the gap between where you stand and the rules you answer to.

    Your program, your policies and your auditor stay yours.

    • Control gap analysis Your environment measured against the controls of your framework.
    • Technical remediation The configuration changes that close the gaps, with the evidence of each.
    • Compliance automation Continuous control monitoring and evidence collection, set up for you.

  • Continuity and infrastructure

    Planning for the unexpected.

    • Disaster recovery and backup Recovery designed for the workspace and its data.
    • Annual recovery test The recovery plan, tested once a year.
    • Business continuity Continuity planning for the managed environment.
    • Network Network design for the workspace, including a private connection to your datacenter.

  • Your sector

    Work specific to financial services and to healthcare.

    For Enterprise clients: customized industry images, designed and quoted for each client.

    • Financial services → Sector-specific work for firms that answer to a regulator.
    • Healthcare → Sector-specific work for organizations that hold patient information.

Questions about projects

  • Can we hire you for one project?

    As the start of Safe Workspace, yes.

    Projects are for Safe Workspace clients: inside an agreement you already have, or as the project that starts one.

  • How is a project priced?

    On its own, before it starts.

    Each project is quoted separately, at a fixed price or on time and materials. It is a line of its own on your invoice, apart from the platform fee.

  • Are add-ons projects?

    Setting one up is.

    Each add-on has a license that recurs and a set-up that happens once. The set-up is scoped and quoted like any other project.

    The add-ons, on Plans →

  • How far does a project reach?

    Your infrastructure, up to the operating system.

    We build and run the infrastructure and operating system your applications need. Your business software, hardware, software development and telecommunications stay with the teams and vendors who own them.
